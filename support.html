Llunari Support

Llunari is a private period calendar for iPhone.

Questions and problems

Write to yung.sun.xxi@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you turned on iCloud sync in Settings. The developer cannot see it.

I got a new iPhone. How do I keep my log?

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on the old iPhone, then install Llunari on the new one with the same Apple Account and turn sync on there.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, "Delete all my data".

Is the fertile window reliable for contraception?

No. It is an estimate from your own log. Llunari is not a medical device and not a method of contraception.

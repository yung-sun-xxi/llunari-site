Llunari Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Llunari is a period calendar for iPhone made by Alexander Stepanenkov. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in it.

What the app stores

The days you mark, the flow and pain you add to them, the answers you give about unusual cycles, and your settings. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, the same data is also kept in your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account, so it can reach your other devices. Apple stores that database; the developer has no access to it and cannot read it.

What the developer receives

Nothing. Llunari has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it contains no third-party code that does.

Face ID

If you turn on the Face ID lock, iOS checks your face and tells the app only whether the check succeeded. The app never receives your face data.

Deleting your data

Settings, "Delete all my data" removes everything the app stores, on the device and, if sync is on, in your iCloud. Deleting the app alone keeps a backup of your log in this iPhone's Keychain, so a reinstall restores it; that backup never leaves the device and is removed by "Delete all my data".

Children

Llunari is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
